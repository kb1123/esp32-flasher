ESP32 Flasher

Install firmware over USB, straight from your phone.

1

Put the board in download mode

  1. Hold the BOOT button on the board.
  2. While holding it, plug the board into your phone with a USB-C OTG cable (or adapter).
  3. Release BOOT after a second.

The board doesn't need Ethernet connected for this step.

2

Connect

Board not in the list?

Check that BOOT was held while plugging in, and that the cable carries data (some charge-only cables don't). You can also list every USB serial device:

3

Choose firmware

Loading firmware list…

Full-flash (merged) images go at 0x0. App-only images go at 0x10000.

4

Install

Connect the board and choose firmware first.

Next

  1. Unplug the USB cable (if the LED isn't blue yet, press RESET).
  2. Plug Ethernet into your router and power the board.
  3. Find its IP in your router's device list (named esp32-ota), or try esp32-ota.local.
  4. Log in with admin / esp32ota and change the password.
